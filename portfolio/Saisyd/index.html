SAISYD CAFE
Saisyd Cafe is a capstone project and web-based system that utilizes the synchronization of a Point-of-Sale (POS) and Inventory Management System. It features both an admin side and a customer side, allowing administrators to manage products, monitor inventory levels, process transactions, and track sales, while customers can browse available products and place orders through an integrated platform.
Sample Website Github

BRANDING

#FFFBF2 serves as the main background color, while the #FFFBF2–#945300 linear gradient adds warmth and depth. #333333 provides strong contrast for text and other elements, #C4A277 introduces a softer accent that complements the primary tones. Finally, #2E1A00 adds a deep brown shade that strengthens contrast and gives the overall design a warm, refined, and harmonious appearance.

FUNCTION

Front-End
The customer-facing side provides a user-friendly interface where customers can browse the café menu, view product details, add items to their cart, and place orders. It also includes a responsive QR code menu designed for convenient access across different devices.

Browse the café menu and view available products.

Order & Checkout

Back-end
The administrative side manages the café’s operations through an integrated POS and inventory management system. Administrators can manage products and ingredients, monitor inventory levels, process orders and payments, track sales, and generate reports to support efficient day-to-day business operations.
