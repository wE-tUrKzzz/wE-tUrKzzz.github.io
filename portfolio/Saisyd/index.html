SAISYD CAFE
Saisyd Cafe is a capstone project and web-based system that utilizes the synchronization of a Point-of-Sale (POS) and Inventory Management System. It features both an admin side and a customer side, allowing administrators to manage products, monitor inventory levels, process transactions, and track sales, while customers can browse available products and place orders through an integrated platform.

BRANDING

#FFFBF2 serves as the primary background color, establishing a clean and inviting foundation that complements the café's overall aesthetic. #FFFBF2–#945300 introduces a warm gradient that adds depth while indicating the cozy and welcoming vibes of the café. #333333 provides contrast for text and other interface elements, ensuring readability and clarity. #C4A277 adds a subtle accent that complements the warmer tones and enhances the visual identity. #2E1A00 introduces a deeper brown shade that strengthens contrast and completes the palette with a refined apperance.

Front-End
The customer-facing interface is designed to provide an accessible and seamless ordering experience, allowing customers to interact with the café's digital platform and complete transactions through an intuitive web-based environment.
